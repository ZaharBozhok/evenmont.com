Questions from partners

Do I need to know Odoo?

No. Technology, training and support are on us. You bring the accounting view — and if you want to learn Odoo, we train your team for free.

Will you do bookkeeping for my client?

No. We never do bookkeeping, tax or financial advisory. The books and the advice stay with you, and the agreement includes a non-solicit clause.

How is the fee paid and disclosed?

10% of the client’s fees in the first 12 months, paid within 30 days after the client pays, with a written disclosure for every fee. Prefer no fee? We pass the same amount to your client as a discount.

What if my client is an attest client?

Use the no-fee option: the amount goes to your client as a discount, and you receive nothing from us. State rules can be stricter — our agreement asks you to confirm yours.

What if the project goes wrong?

Each phase has a fixed price, a founder is on every project, and weekly demos surface problems early. You see the status in the partner portal. The client signs the implementation contract with us, not with you.

Can I refer clients who aren’t ready yet?

Yes. Register the introduction and it is protected for 12 months. If Odoo isn’t a fit yet, we tell your client so.

Do you work with QuickBooks Online clients?

Yes. Migration from QuickBooks is part of every Launch package: customers, vendors, items, opening balances and history, with a cutover date agreed with you.

I’m already in Odoo’s program for accounting firms — why you?

The two fit together: you advise, we implement. We do the configuration, migration, integrations, training and support for your client, while the books and the advice stay with you.
