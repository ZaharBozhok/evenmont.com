## Before

Every custom sign was quoted by the owner from memory and a price list. Jobs moved through the shop on paper travelers, sheet metal was counted by eye and scrap was never tracked. Quotes took up to two days, and some jobs were won at a loss.

## Why they chose us

They wanted quoting and job tracking without a heavy ERP. We showed them the shop-floor flow in the fit call and priced the Blueprint up front.

## What we did

### Blueprint

We rebuilt ten past quotes from real material and labor costs and found where margin leaked. Fixed price for two phases.

### Build

Product templates with bills of materials for their standard sign types, quotes built from them, work orders by stage, sheet stock and remnants tracked in inventory, and job costing into the books.

### Go-live

Sales team first, then the shop floor. 30 days of hypercare.

### Keep

Managed Odoo and quarterly pricing reviews.
