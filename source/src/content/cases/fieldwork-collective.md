## Before

Hours lived in one tool, invoices in another, field contractors sent their bills by email, and the books were closed from spreadsheet exports. Project leads found out whether a project made money only after it was over — usually a month later.

## Why they chose us

A fixed price for each phase, their accountant in the loop from the first week, and a founder on the project from the Blueprint to go-live. They had already tried a larger partner who quoted by the hour and never gave a total.

## What we did

### Blueprint

Two weeks mapping how work is sold, staffed, billed and paid. We measured the baseline — 11 days to close, 71% of hours invoiced in the month — and agreed a fixed price for the build. Their accountant owned the chart of accounts and signed off on the setup.

### Build

CRM, projects and timesheets first, then invoicing from approved hours and our contractor module for rates, contractor invoices and payouts. A demo every Friday, with the project leads in the room.

### Go-live

Opening balances agreed with the accountant, the team trained on the real setup, and 30 days of hypercare. Timesheet approval moved to the project leads on day one.

### Keep

Managed Odoo: support, updates and one small improvement every month.
