## Before

Engineers logged time in a standalone tracker, project managers rebuilt invoices by hand from exports, and retainers were tracked in a shared spreadsheet. About one hour in ten never made it onto an invoice, and subcontractors were paid late because nobody could match their bills to the work.

## Why they chose us

They wanted a partner who understood billable work and could show the numbers before signing anything. The Blueprint gave them a measured baseline, a fixed price and a plan their controller agreed with.

## What we did

### Blueprint

We traced one month of hours from timesheet to invoice to payment and found where time went missing. Fixed price agreed for two build phases.

### Build

Timesheets by project phase, invoicing from approved hours, retainer tracking and our contractor module for subcontractor rates, bills and payouts. Documents attached to each project, not to email.

### Go-live

Historic open invoices migrated, project managers trained first, then the whole team. 30 days of hypercare.

### Keep

Managed Odoo with a monthly review of unbilled hours.
