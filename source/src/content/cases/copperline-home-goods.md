## Before

Shopify, Amazon and wholesale orders each lived in their own tool. Stock was counted in spreadsheets, oversells happened every week, and the bookkeeper spent two days a week matching payouts to QuickBooks. Nobody could say with confidence what a SKU really cost after freight and duties.

## Why they chose us

Fixed-price phases and a Blueprint that measured their stock accuracy and fulfilment speed before any build. Their accountant set up inventory valuation and COGS with us, so the books would match the warehouse from day one.

## What we did

### Blueprint

We walked the warehouse, mapped every order channel and measured the baseline: 86% stock accuracy, 64% of orders shipped the same day.

### Build

Inventory with barcode scanning and bin locations, purchase with landed costs, sales tax through Avalara, and storefront connectors for Shopify and Amazon. Weekly demos on the warehouse floor.

### Go-live

A full count the weekend before launch, opening balances agreed with the accountant, pickers trained on the scanners, 30 days of hypercare through the holiday peak.

### Keep

Managed Odoo, with connector monitoring every morning.
