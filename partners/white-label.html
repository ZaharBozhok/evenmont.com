Questions from agencies and Odoo partners

Will your team talk to our client?

Only if you want us to. We work under your brand, from your email and channels, and contact your client directly only with your permission.

Who owns the code?

All code is assigned to you or your client.

How do you price white-label work?

A fixed price per scope, or a monthly capacity block, with payment by milestones. You get a fixed quote before you send your proposal.

How do you fit into our process?

We work in your tools and tracker, send daily async updates, go through your code review, write documentation and follow Odoo coding guidelines.

Can you take over a stuck project?

Yes — rescuing stuck projects is one of the reasons teams call us. Send us the scope and the current state, and we come back with a fixed quote.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes, before we see any client details.

How many projects can you take on?

We take 2 white-label projects at a time — currently up to two people per project.
