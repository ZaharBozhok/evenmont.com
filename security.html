Security & data

Your data, your choice.

Where your data lives, who can see it, and how it is protected during and after the project.

  • Where your data lives

    On Odoo.sh or on your own server — your choice.

  • Who has access

    Named accounts, least privilege and MFA. Access is removed after the project.

  • Backups

    Backups are in place, and we test a restore before go-live.

  • NDA

    We sign an NDA on request.

  • GDPR

    As an EU-based company, we process personal data in line with GDPR.

  • Security questions

    Write to us at security@evenmont.com.
